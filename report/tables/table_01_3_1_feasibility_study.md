# Table 1: Feasibility study

From the final report, section "3.1 Feasibility study".

| Dimension | Finding | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical | C++17 with SQLite is suitable; the system can run offline on one computer or a local network | Test with 800 students on the school's computers |
| Operational | Four roles with clear jobs; a moderation step is needed to resolve the Principal and lecturer conflict; menus must be simple for non-IT staff | Provide role-specific instructions and training |
| Economic | No extra services are needed; no project budget was given, so no costs are invented | The school must confirm budget, hardware and support staff before real use |
| Legal | The system holds personal data of children; the Data Protection Act 2012, Children's Protection and Welfare Act 2011 and Persons with Disability Equity Act 2021 apply | Re-check legal requirements before deployment |
| Schedule | Seven weeks is possible but tight, because phases depend on each other and implementation cannot start before Gate 2 | Watch the schedule and manage risks |
